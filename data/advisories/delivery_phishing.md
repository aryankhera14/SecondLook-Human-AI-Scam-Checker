Victims of such phishing scams would receive spoofed e-mails or text messages purportedly sent from a delivery company such as SingPost and DHL.
Do not click on URL links provided in unsolicited emails and text messages.
Verify the authenticity of the information with the official website or sources.
Contact your bank immediately if you receive an unexpected OTP.
Never disclose your personal or banking details or OTPs to anyone.
