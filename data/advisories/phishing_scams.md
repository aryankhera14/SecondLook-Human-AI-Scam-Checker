Phishing scams involve emails, messages, calls, or advertisements by scammers posing as government officials, financial institutions or businesses.
Victims would be tricked into revealing sensitive information such as usernames, passwords, banking credentials and/or debit or credit card information by clicking malicious links or via phone calls.
Clickable links from unsolicited/Random emails, messages, images, or advertisements
Spoofed website addresses
Requests for your One-Time passwords (OTPs), personal or banking details
Prompts you to download attachments or third-party apps outside official app stores
Call your bank immediately and make a police report if you think you have fallen victim.
