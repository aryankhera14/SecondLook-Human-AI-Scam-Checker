Job scams typically involve victims being offered online jobs that could be performed from home.
Scammers ask victims to perform tasks for commission, such as making advance purchases, liking social media posts, or completing surveys.
Another scheme involves the transfer of funds to bank accounts provided by the scammers, for a small commission.
It is likely a scam if the job requires you to: Pay upfront before starting the job.
It is likely a scam if the job requires you to use your own money to complete tasks.
It is likely a scam if the job requires you to use your personal bank account as part of the job.
Legitimate employers will never ask for your Singpass credentials and password for job applications.
Call the ScamShield helpline (1799) to check if you are unsure.
