title: Government official impersonation scams (ScamShield)
source_url: https://www.scamshield.gov.sg/i-want-protection-from-scams/learn-to-recognise-scams/government-officials-impersonation-scams/
applies_to: government
---
Victims would receive unsolicited phone calls or in-app calls (e.g. WhatsApp video call) allegedly from government agencies.
The scammers would accuse the victims of being involved in criminal activities.
Victims would then be instructed by the scammers to transfer money into a 'safe account'.
Government officials will NEVER do these things over a phone call: Ask you to transfer money; Ask you to disclose bank log-in details; Ask you to install mobile apps from unofficial app stores; Transfer your call to Police
Call the ScamShield Helpline (1799) to check if you are unsure.
