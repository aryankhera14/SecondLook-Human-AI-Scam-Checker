Suspicious Sender ID: messages from random phone numbers rather than official sender IDs.
Fraudulent Links: URLs that don't match the organisation's official domain.
Urgent Language: pressure tactics like "Immediate action required!" or "Your account will be locked!"
Personal Information Requests: asking for banking details or passwords via SMS.
Poor Grammar/Spelling: professional organisations maintain quality writing standards.
Unsolicited Attachments or Payment Requests, especially lottery or fee-based claims.
All official government SMS messages come from the gov.sg sender ID as of 1 July 2024.
Use the ScamShield app or helpline (1799) to check or report suspicious texts.
