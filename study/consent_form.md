# Consent form: SecondLook user study (CS619 course project)

**What you'll do (about 30 minutes):** read 8 short text messages and decide what you would do about each one. For 4 messages you'll decide on your own; for the other 4 you'll see an AI assistant's verdict first. Please think aloud while you work.

**What is recorded:** your decision for each message, a 1–5 confidence rating, the time taken, and the researcher's written notes of what you say. No audio or video is recorded unless you agree below. No names are stored; you're identified only by a code such as P1.

**The messages are not real.** They were written to resemble common scam patterns described in official advisories. You will not be asked to click anything or share any personal details. One AI verdict is deliberately wrong; we'll explain which one at the end.

**Your rights:** taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and ask for your data to be deleted. Data is used only for the CS619 course project report and deleted after grading.
